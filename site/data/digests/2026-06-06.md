**Mistral launches enterprise-focused AI platform with Mistral 3**
Europe's leading AI company unveiled Mistral 3, positioning it as "the most powerful AI platform for enterprises." The new offering lets companies customize, fine-tune, and deploy AI assistants and autonomous agents using open models. This is Mistral's direct challenge to OpenAI's enterprise dominance, betting that businesses want more control over their AI infrastructure than closed models allow.
Why it matters: European companies worried about data sovereignty now have a serious alternative to American AI platforms. If Mistral can match ChatGPT's capabilities while keeping data local, expect a wave of enterprise switches across the EU.

**Anthropic's token counting bug cost users money**
Anthropic engineer Thibault Sottiaux revealed the company fixed a "codex bug" that was undercounting tokens served to Pro and Plus subscribers. The bug affected less than 15% of accounts but meant some users were charged for more usage than the system was tracking. Sottiaux called it "not the kind of bug you want us to fix" but said transparency was important.
Why it matters: If Anthropic doesn't know exactly how many tokens it's serving, no AI company does. Every startup building "unlimited AI" pricing is flying blind on usage patterns, which means those prices are about to change.
