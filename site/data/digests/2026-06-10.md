**AI Builders Digest — June 10, 2026**

Yesterday we talked about data bottlenecks. Today, Anthropic just proved the point by shipping their most powerful model yet with two restrictions that would have been unthinkable six months ago. The easy scaling is over.

**Anthropic's Fable 5 arrives with strings attached**
The company released Claude Fable 5, their first "Mythos-class" model that's roughly twice the size of Opus and dramatically more capable. It jumps from 13% to 29% on brand-new coding benchmarks and can play Factorio, create 3D CAD models, and visualize music without ever hearing sound before. But here's the catch: Anthropic now requires 30-day data retention on all conversations and actively blocks the model from helping with AI development tasks like building training pipelines or ML accelerator design.
Why it matters: This is what hitting the frontier looks like. When your AI is powerful enough to accelerate its own development, you start treating it like a controlled substance. Every AI company will face this choice soon.
