**AI Builders Digest — June 13, 2026**

Box CEO Aaron Levie just dropped the most comprehensive survey data on AI adoption we've seen yet. The results flip the "AI will kill jobs" narrative on its head, but they also reveal something more interesting: the companies winning with AI aren't slowing down. They're doubling down.

**Companies using AI most are hiring the most, not firing**
Box CEO Aaron Levie shared results from a survey of 1,640 IT leaders across the US, Japan, and Europe about AI agent adoption. The standout finding: companies that adopted AI the most are planning to grow headcount the most. Levie called it "quite intuitive that the companies that become most productive want to (and are able to) reinvest back into the business to keep getting the gains going."
Why it matters: This is the first large-scale data showing AI creates a productivity flywheel, not a job displacement crisis. If you work at a company that's slow to adopt AI, you're not safer from layoffs — you're at a company that can't afford to grow.

**Replit CEO says Fable 5 economics finally work for agents**
Replit CEO Amjad Masad praised his team for making Anthropic's Fable model "cost stomachable" in Replit Agent. His key insight: "The lack of mistakes net net makes it more affordable." Better accuracy means fewer expensive retry loops.
Why it matters: This is the first public confirmation that Fable 5's improved reliability translates to real cost savings for AI agent companies. Every startup burning cash on model API calls just got a lifeline.

**Vercel CEO highlights 500 orders in 2 minutes using AI-built store**
Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch showcased a custom Shopify storefront that processed 500+ orders in 2 minutes. The entire store was built using v0 and Cursor AI on a headless Next.js setup. "Anyone can now dream → build → ship → sell," Rauch wrote.
Why it matters: This is what happens when AI coding tools mature. Building e-commerce is becoming as easy as setting up a WordPress blog was in 2010. If you sell physical products and don't have an online store yet, you're about to have 10,000 new AI-powered competitors.
