**AI Builders Digest — June 13, 2026**

Three days after launching, Anthropic's most powerful models just got pulled offline by the US government. Meanwhile, OpenAI is quietly buying the infrastructure to make sure their AI agents never go down.

**US government forces Anthropic to suspend Fable and Mythos models**
Just 72 hours after release, Anthropic had to pull its newest Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models offline following a US government directive. The suspension affects all customers worldwide, not just government users. Anthropic says the order was based on a "potential narrow, non-universal jailbreak" that the company disputes, noting similar capabilities exist in other models including GPT-5.5.
Why it matters: This is the first time the US government has forced an AI company to revoke models from paying customers based on security concerns. Every AI lab is now wondering which of their capabilities might trigger the next shutdown.

**OpenAI acquires cloud infrastructure startup Ona**
OpenAI announced plans to acquire Ona to expand its Codex platform with secure, persistent cloud environments. The acquisition will enable long-running AI agents that can work across enterprise workflows without interruption.
Why it matters: While competitors deal with government shutdowns, OpenAI is building the plumbing for AI agents that enterprises can actually rely on. Persistent environments solve the "why did my agent forget everything?" problem that's plagued business AI deployments.
https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-ona

**Google DeepMind launches $10M multi-agent safety research fund**
The company announced a new funding call specifically for research into multi-agent AI safety. The initiative comes as more companies deploy multiple AI systems that need to work together reliably.
Why it matters: Google is betting that the future of AI involves multiple agents collaborating, which creates entirely new failure modes. The company that figures out multi-agent safety first will have a major advantage in enterprise deployments.
https://deepmind.google/blog/investing-in-multi-agent-ai-safety-research/

**Replit CEO shows off unified development canvas**
Replit CEO Amjad Masad shared a demo of building an entire company on one canvas: web app, mobile app, marketing materials, and App Store assets all in the same workspace. Users can click into any component and start building or generating new features immediately.
Why it matters: This is Replit's answer to the "context switching kills productivity" problem. If you can build your entire stack without leaving one interface, that's a genuine workflow improvement over juggling dozens of tools.
