**AI Builders Digest — May 21, 2026**

Enterprise CFOs are about to discover what crypto miners learned years ago: when your business model runs on tokens, every click costs money. And the solutions companies are trying would make a spreadsheet auditor weep.

**Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic**
The former Tesla AI director and OpenAI co-founder announced he's joining Anthropic to focus on R&D. Karpathy called the next few years at the frontier of large language models "especially formative" and said he plans to resume his educational work later.
Why it matters: Karpathy built the neural networks that taught Tesla cars to drive and co-founded the company that created ChatGPT. When someone with that resume says the next few years will be "formative," he's not talking about incremental improvements.

**Google launches Gemini Spark, its first proactive AI agent**
Josh Woodward from Google announced Gemini Spark, a 24/7 personal AI agent designed to proactively manage tasks and navigate digital life. It's rolling out to trusted testers this week and will be available as a beta for US Google AI Ultra subscribers next week.
Why it matters: Google is betting that people want an AI that acts without being asked. If you've ever wished your phone would automatically reschedule meetings when you're stuck in traffic, this is that future. The question is whether "proactive" feels helpful or creepy.
https://x.com/joshwoodward/status/2056873495116845485

**Fortune 500 CIOs are panicking about AI token costs**
Box CEO Aaron Levie shared insights from a dinner with Fortune 500 CIOs where token costs dominated the conversation. Companies are trying everything: different models for different workloads, tiered access by user type, team spending caps, and use-case justifications. But according to Levie, "basically no one feels like they have the right solution."
Why it matters: Your IT department is about to start tracking AI usage like they track printer paper. The companies figuring out token economics first will have a massive cost advantage over those still giving everyone unlimited access to GPT-4.
https://x.com/levie/status/2056965292753146019

**Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash posts strong coding benchmark scores**
Investor Matt Turck highlighted Google's latest model performance, noting major improvements in coding tasks. Gemini 3.5 Flash scored 76.2% on Terminal-Bench for coding agents and 56.5% on Toolathon for real-world tasks, with leading scores across multiple coding benchmarks including SWE-Bench.
Why it matters: Google was considered behind in the AI race just months ago. These benchmarks suggest they're not just catching up on coding tasks, they might be pulling ahead.
