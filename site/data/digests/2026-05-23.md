**AI Builders Digest — May 23, 2026**

Microsoft just shipped three AI tools designed to run on your laptop instead of their cloud. While everyone else is building bigger models that need more servers, Redmond is betting small models with better orchestration can handle most of what people actually want AI to do.

**Microsoft's answer to local AI agents arrives**
Microsoft Research released MagenticLite, an AI agent system that works across your browser and local files without sending data to the cloud. It's powered by two custom small models: MagenticBrain for reasoning and file tasks, and Fara1.5 for web browsing. The system is designed around a key bet that AI capability comes from tool orchestration, not just model size.
Why it matters: This is the first serious attempt at a full-featured AI agent that keeps your data local. If it works well, every company worried about sending sensitive information to ChatGPT or Claude now has an alternative that runs entirely on their own hardware.
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/magenticlite-magenticbrain-fara1-5-an-agentic-experience-optimized-for-small-models/

**Neural's new design hits all platforms**
Josh Woodward from Neural shared excitement about user reactions to the company's new "Neural Expressive" design, now available across iOS, Android, and web. The interface update appears to be getting positive feedback from early users.
Why it matters: Neural is positioning itself as a cross-platform AI interface, and design consistency across mobile and web is crucial for user adoption in a crowded AI app market.

**Replit offers revenue sharing for app developers**
Replit CEO Amjad Masad announced that developers can now monetize apps built on the platform and receive credit rewards in return. The program appears designed to encourage more commercial development on Replit's infrastructure.
Why it matters: Replit is trying to become the go-to platform for AI-powered apps by sharing revenue with developers. If successful, this could shift where the next generation of AI applications gets built and hosted.
