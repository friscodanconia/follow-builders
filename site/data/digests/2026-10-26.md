**Peter Yang on the only kids' learning app his family actually stuck with**

Product builder Peter Yang shared why Once Reading is the only education app that his four-year-old has consistently used for months. The key mechanics: daily parent-child habit, lightweight gamification like taking a silly photo after each lesson, and physical books mailed to the child after 30 lessons to create a real sense of achievement.

Why it matters: Most AI-adjacent education apps chase engagement with streaks and badges and never graduate kids to anything real. Once Reading ships actual books. The lesson for anyone building AI-assisted learning tools is that the physical handoff might matter more than the software.
