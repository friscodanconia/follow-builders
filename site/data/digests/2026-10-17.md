**Box CEO Aaron Levie: background agents will consume 100x to 1000x more tokens than anything you've seen**

Box CEO Aaron Levie laid out the arithmetic on where AI token consumption is headed. The current model, where you type a prompt and wait for a response, is tiny compared to what comes next: agents running in the background, autonomously moving data between systems, reviewing documents, scanning code for security issues, processing logs. Levie argues that most business work today is rate-limited by human capacity to process information, and agents are about to remove that ceiling entirely.

Why it matters: If your company is budgeting for AI based on current usage, you're budgeting for the wrong order of magnitude. The teams signing "unlimited AI" contracts right now are going to have a very interesting conversation with their CFO when background agents start running 24 hours a day against every dataset you own.

**Anthropic is running a public competition to find prompt injection attacks on Claude**

Boris Cherny at Anthropic announced a competition with Gray Swan to surface prompt injection vulnerabilities in Claude models. Prompt injection is when a bad actor hides instructions inside content that an AI agent reads, essentially hijacking the agent mid-task to do something its operator didn't intend. Anthropic says it's made progress on this over the past few years but calls it "increasingly urgent" as agents take on more autonomous work.

Why it matters: The timing is telling. Anthropic doesn't run public bug bounties for solved problems. If you're deploying Claude-based agents against customer data or internal systems, "prompt injection" belongs in your security review right now, not after an incident. And if you're a security researcher, there's a competition worth entering.
