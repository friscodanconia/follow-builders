**AI Builders Digest — May 29, 2026**

Microsoft just solved the messy reality of enterprise AI: how to connect agents to actual company data instead of toy demos. Meanwhile, the European expansion race is heating up as AI labs chase regulatory advantages.

**Microsoft's Data Formulator 0.7 connects AI agents to real enterprise data**
Microsoft Research released Data Formulator 0.7, an open-source system that finally tackles the unsexy problem of getting AI agents to work with enterprise data. The tool connects databases, warehouses, and BI systems in one workspace, then lets AI agents analyze and visualize data without requiring SQL skills. Teams can now run complex analytics across fragmented data sources with no coding required.
Why it matters: Every company trying to deploy AI agents hits the same wall: connecting them to real company data scattered across dozens of systems. Microsoft just open-sourced the plumbing that makes agent workflows actually work in enterprise environments.
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/data-formulator-0-7-ai-powered-data-analytics-for-enterprise-data/

**Mistral ships Small 4 for autonomous enterprise agents**
France's Mistral AI released Mistral Small 4, positioning it specifically for enterprises building autonomous agents and multimodal AI systems. The company is doubling down on its enterprise platform strategy with customization and fine-tuning capabilities.
Why it matters: While OpenAI and Google fight over consumer pricing, Mistral is carving out the "AI that stays in Europe" niche. For multinational companies dealing with GDPR and data sovereignty rules, that regulatory moat is worth paying for.
https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-small-4/

**OpenAI showcases Endava's "agentic organization" with Codex**
OpenAI highlighted how consulting firm Endava uses Codex to build what they call an "agentic organization." The company cut requirements analysis from weeks to hours and accelerated overall software delivery using AI agents for project workflows.
Why it matters: This is what "AI transformation" looks like in practice. Not replacing developers, but rebuilding how consulting firms scope and deliver projects. If a 11,000-person company can restructure around agents, smaller firms are about to face some very aggressive competition.
https://openai.com/index/endava
