**Stop prompting your coding agents manually**
PSPDFKit founder Peter Steinberger put it bluntly: "You shouldn't be prompting coding agents anymore. You should be designing loops that prompt your agents." His post sparked over 1,000 replies from developers sharing their own automation setups. The message is clear — if you're still typing prompts into ChatGPT for code, you're working like it's 2023.
Why it matters: Manual prompting doesn't scale when you're burning through millions of tokens monthly. The developers building automated agent workflows now will have a massive productivity edge over those still doing it by hand.

**Vercel's AI Gateway saves 1 trillion tokens monthly through smart retries**
Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch revealed their AI Gateway recovers over 1 trillion tokens per month by automatically retrying failed API calls to OpenAI, Anthropic, and other providers. The service adds redundancy and observability with zero markup over the original model prices — essentially free money for developers whose apps would otherwise crash when AI APIs go down.
Why it matters: Your AI app probably fails silently more than you realize. Every failed API call is wasted tokens and frustrated users. Companies like Vercel are building the reliability layer that most startups don't want to build themselves.

**Box CEO Aaron Levie: AI workloads will split into two tiers**
Aaron Levie predicts AI use cases will stratify between expensive frontier models for complex work and cheap models for high-volume tasks that don't need GPT-4 level intelligence. The big shift: the middle layer that intelligently routes tasks between models is becoming the most valuable part of the stack.
Why it matters: If your AI strategy is still "use the best model for everything," you're about to get priced out. The companies that master model routing will cut costs by 80% while maintaining quality.
