**Claude Projects brings persistent memory to coding workflows**

Thariq, who works at Anthropic, described a meaningful architectural update: Claude Projects now gives each project a dedicated agent that manages memory and spins off subagents for specific tasks. You can set it to be proactive, schedule work, and pick up context across sessions instead of starting fresh each time.

Boris Cherny, also at Anthropic, backed this up from personal use: he stopped managing sessions entirely. Thoughts go in as they come, Claude organizes them into threads, and the project learns how he works over time.

Why it matters: Session management is one of those invisible taxes developers pay constantly. Every time you start a new conversation with a coding assistant, you spend the first few exchanges re-explaining your codebase, your preferences, your constraints. A persistent project agent that remembers all of that is a real productivity change, not a marginal one. If this works as described, the people who adopt it early will look unreasonably fast to everyone else.
