**OpenAI reveals how it runs Codex without breaking everything**
The company published its internal security playbook for running Codex, their AI coding agent. The setup includes sandboxed environments, human approval workflows for sensitive operations, network policies that prevent agents from accessing production systems, and what they call "agent-native telemetry" to track what the AI is actually doing in real time.
Why it matters: Every company trying to deploy AI coding tools has been reinventing these same security controls. OpenAI just open-sourced the blueprint, which means your IT team's concerns about "rogue AI agents" now have documented solutions.

Why it matters: This is Anthropic's play for the most AI-cautious industry on the planet. If banks and insurers start using Claude agents for compliance and risk analysis, that's proof the technology cleared the highest security bar in corporate America.
