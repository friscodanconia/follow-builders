**AI Builders Digest — May 26, 2026**

The C-suite is discovering what their engineers already know: AI demos are magic, AI production is messy. And the gap between "look what I built in 10 minutes" and "this actually works reliably" is wider than anyone wants to admit.

**Box CEO Aaron Levie calls out executive "AI psychosis"**
Levie posted a reality check that's been needed for months: CEOs are prone to AI delusions because they're too far from the actual work. They see the happy path demos but miss the 10 or 20 steps needed to make AI agents work sustainably. "Look I made this awesome product prototype" — yes, but you didn't have to review the code before production or fix the inevitable issues.
Why it matters: Every startup that hired 3 people and gave them AI agents instead of hiring 10 is about to learn this the hard way. The work didn't disappear. It moved to the person who has to babysit the agent.
https://x.com/levie/status/2058582370253701432

**The new startup playbook: build the system that builds your MVP**
Product manager Peter Yang interviewed entrepreneur Ryan Carson, who runs his entire startup solo using AI agents. Carson uses OpenClaw as his AI chief of staff for email triage and sales outreach, plus Codex and Devin as his engineering team that ships features while he sleeps. His advice: "Spend a lot of time upfront setting up your skills and documentation. Then you've suddenly unlocked the work of 10 people."
Why it matters: This is what happens when AI tools cross the reliability threshold. Solo founders can now compete with funded teams, but only if they invest heavily in the setup work most people skip.
https://x.com/petergyang/status/2058555226479866312

**Chinese AI company MiniMax launches Hailuo 02 video model**
MiniMax released their latest video generation model, claiming native 1080p output and "extreme physics mastery." The company says they've generated 370 million videos and rank #2 globally on some benchmarks.
Why it matters: Another Chinese lab claiming to match or beat Western video models, but at a fraction of the cost. If the quality claims hold up, this puts more pressure on OpenAI's Sora pricing when it finally launches.
