**Microsoft builds an AI model just for power grids**
Microsoft Research released GridSFM, a small foundation model that can solve complex power grid optimization problems in milliseconds. The model helps grid operators figure out the cheapest way to dispatch electricity while keeping the system stable. Microsoft says the decisions this model helps with directly impact $20 billion per year in congestion costs and massive amounts of wasted renewable energy.
Why it matters: This is AI solving real infrastructure problems, not generating more content. Every utility company struggling to integrate solar and wind power just got a tool that could save them millions. Expect more of these industry-specific models.
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/gridsfm-a-new-small-foundation-model-for-the-electric-grid/
