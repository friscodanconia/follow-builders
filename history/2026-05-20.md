**Box CEO Aaron Levie explains why your AI agents keep failing**
Box CEO Aaron Levie posted a detailed thread about the real reason enterprise AI agents break: terrible data hygiene. "The biggest challenge that most companies run into in their agent strategy is getting agents the right constrained context," he wrote. Too much conflicting information, outdated documents, and tribal knowledge systems confuse agents into producing wrong results.
Why it matters: Every company that thought they could just point an AI agent at their existing knowledge base is learning this lesson expensively. The agents aren't the problem, your decade of messy documentation is.

**Developer Thariq shares the prompt that's going viral**
Developer Thariq posted a refined version of his implementation prompt after it gained significant traction. The prompt instructs AI to maintain running "implementation-notes.html" files that capture design decisions, deviations from specs, and tradeoffs made during coding projects.
Why it matters: This is what good AI-assisted development looks like in practice. The prompt creates accountability and documentation that most developers skip, making the AI actually make you better at your job.
