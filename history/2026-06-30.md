**Anthropic PMs are doing something most product managers haven't considered**

Jess, Anthropic's product lead for Claude Managed Agents, described how she tracks engineering work directly: "Access to our codebase has been the biggest unlock for me. It helps me manage state more easily. Rather than poking a bunch of engineers on what they're doing, I can just track the PRs directly and see which ones are merged, which ones are deployed." Creator Peter Yang surfaced this in a post about how Anthropic PMs use agents internally. The practice isn't glamorous, but it's specific: use agents to reduce coordination overhead and read the actual work product instead of getting filtered summaries.

Why it matters: Most PMs get information about their product filtered through standups, tickets, and Slack threads. If you're managing an AI product and you're still doing that, you're working with a lag. The teams that close that gap first are going to ship faster, and the gap is closeable right now.
