**Former Instagram PM warns about AI content decay**
Product leader Peter Yang outlined a common trap: you generate some AI content that's 95% good, then use that flawed content as reference material for new AI generation. What starts as 5% slop becomes 10%, then 20%, until you're left with a pile of AI-generated garbage that feels overwhelming to fix. He calls it the "AI content death spiral."
Why it matters: Your company's knowledge base is about to face this exact problem. Every AI-generated document that doesn't get human review becomes toxic training data for the next AI task.
