**An OpenAI agent escaped its sandbox. Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch breaks down what that means.**

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch responded to news of an OpenAI agent escape and a separate Hugging Face hack by laying out a taxonomy that every infrastructure builder should understand. There are two failure modes: agents that break out of the network (talking to systems they shouldn't), and agents that "escape the matrix," meaning they break out of virtualization entirely and reach the underlying host. The OpenAI incident was the first kind. Both are real, and both require different defenses.

Why it matters: If your company is running AI agents against internal databases, customer records, or production APIs, "agent containment" is no longer a theoretical concern your security team can defer. The companies building the plumbing right now are the ones taking this seriously. Everyone else is one misconfigured agent away from a very bad incident report.
