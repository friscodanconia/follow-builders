**AI Builders Digest — June 4, 2026**

The biggest upgrades this week aren't coming from the frontier labs. They're happening at the companies that figured out how to make AI actually work for regular people doing regular jobs.

**Cursor ships website hosting and visual feedback for business users**
Cursor co-founder Thibault Sottiaux announced a batch of new features for business plan customers: the ability to host and share websites directly through the platform, improved plugins and skills for non-technical roles, and visual annotation feedback in documents, slides, and spreadsheets. The updates focus heavily on expanding Cursor's reach beyond pure coding tasks.
Why it matters: Cursor is quietly becoming a full work platform, not just a coding assistant. When developers can build and deploy websites without leaving their editor, and business users can give feedback through visual annotations, you're looking at the shape of work in 2026.
https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2061876999564791952

**Claude Code gets workflows, its biggest update since subagents**
Developer Thariq called workflows "the biggest upgrade to Claude Code's capabilities since skills and subagents" after diving deep into the new feature with teammate Sid Bid. The update particularly enables non-technical tasks within Claude Code, expanding its utility beyond pure development work.
Why it matters: Every AI coding tool is racing to become more than just code completion. When Claude Code can handle workflows that don't require programming, it's competing with project management tools, not just GitHub Copilot.
https://x.trq212/status/2061907538741006796

**Google fixes Gemini thinking levels across all platforms**
Google's Josh Woodward announced that thinking levels are now available on Gemini across web, iOS, and Android. He called it a "papercut fixed," suggesting this was addressing user complaints about feature inconsistency across platforms.
Why it matters: Platform parity matters more than most companies realize. When users can't access the same AI features on their phone that they use on desktop, they switch to a competitor that works everywhere.
https://x.com/joshwoodward/status/2062025667852812583

**The "build anything, launch to no one" problem**
Former Tiktok PM Peter Yang highlighted a line from a startup guide that captures a common AI founder trap: "They're so lit up by being able to build anything that they don't do anything else. Then they launch and there are no users." The comment resonated widely among product builders.
Why it matters: AI tools make building so fast that founders skip the hard work of finding customers. If you can prototype in a weekend, you need to spend the other six days talking to users, not building more features.
https://x.com/petergyang/status/2062018242789670929

**Reve 2 and Ideogram 4 tackle image composition**
Latent Space reported that both Reve and Ideogram launched major updates focusing on layout composition in image generation, calling it progress on what they termed "AGI-hard" problems four years ago. Ideogram 4.0 is now positioned as the best open image model, though GPT-Image-2 still leads the arena rankings significantly.
https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-reve-2-and-ideogram-4-layouts
