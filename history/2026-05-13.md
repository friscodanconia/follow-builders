**AI Builders Digest — May 13, 2026**

OpenAI just killed fine-tuning APIs, and the industry's response reveals which companies were building on solid ground versus riding the optimization hype train. Meanwhile, the real action is happening in specialized deployment: Anthropic is shipping finance-specific agents while Google talks up its coding assistant's impact across entire fields.

**OpenAI kills fine-tuning, reveals who was actually building products**
Latent Space Newsletter reports that OpenAI has deprecated their fine-tuning APIs, ending years of "get o1 performance at 4o prices" promises that dominated AI engineering talks. The move comes as Anthropic may raise at a higher valuation than OpenAI for the first time. Interestingly, top-tier companies like Cursor and Cognition have increased their open model fine-tuning rather than relying on hosted APIs.
Why it matters: Every startup that built their moat around fine-tuning OpenAI models just lost their competitive advantage. The companies still standing are the ones who invested in open models and custom infrastructure from the beginning.
https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-the-end-of-finetuning

**Anthropic ships Claude agents built for banks and insurance**
The company released ten new plugins for financial services, including Cowork and Claude Code integrations with Microsoft 365, plus new connectors and an MCP app designed specifically for finance and insurance organizations. No flashy demo, just tools that work within existing enterprise workflows.
Why it matters: While everyone else builds general-purpose agents, Anthropic is winning specific industries by solving actual compliance and integration problems. Your bank's next chatbot upgrade will likely run on this.
https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents

**Google's AlphaEvolve claims broad impact across industries**
Google DeepMind published a blog post about AlphaEvolve, their Gemini-powered coding agent, highlighting its applications across business, infrastructure, and scientific research. The post positions the tool as driving measurable impact beyond typical coding assistance.
Why it matters: Google is making the same bet as GitHub Copilot, but for entire business processes instead of individual developers. If it works, your company's next digital transformation project might involve AI agents writing the code.
https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/
