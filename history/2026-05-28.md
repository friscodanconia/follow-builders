**Replit CEO Amjad Masad honored by Jordan's King for AI contributions**
Amjad Masad received a medal from King Abdullah II for Distinction on Jordan's 80th Independence Day. The Replit founder reflected on his journey building the company from Jordan over 15 years ago and helping accelerate agentic AI globally.
Why it matters: This recognition shows how seriously governments are taking AI leadership, especially from companies building the infrastructure that lets anyone code with AI agents.

**Zara Zhang's slide generator hits 19,000 GitHub stars with major upgrade**
The Frontend Slides skill got a "design brain" that can pull from Zhang's Beautiful HTML Templates library, pick visual directions, and generate slides using specific design languages. New features include better compatibility with coding agents beyond Claude Code, export to web or PDF, and inline editing.
Why it matters: This is what the future of design tools looks like. Instead of choosing from templates, you describe what you want and the AI picks the right design system and builds it for you.
https://x.com/zarazhangrui/status/2059338915023393161

**PSPDFKit's Peter Steinberger releases image processing library for AI agents**
Steinberger extracted his company's image logic into Rastermill, a Node.js library built with WebAssembly and Rust. It's designed to prevent small corrupted images from crashing AI agent processes.
Why it matters: Someone finally built the unglamorous infrastructure AI agents need. If your agent processes user uploads, this prevents the "one bad image kills the entire workflow" problem that nobody talks about.
https://x.com/steipete/status/2059423344961671290
