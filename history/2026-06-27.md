**Vercel's Guillermo Rauch on teaching AI agents your company's taste**

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch shared a post on how his team encodes design standards directly into coding agents, so the AI ships UI that matches Vercel's visual identity rather than generic output. The approach appears to involve structured documentation or prompting that acts as a style guide the agent references during code generation.

Why it matters: Most teams using coding agents are getting functionally correct output that looks like it came from a template. Rauch's framing suggests the real competitive edge is in the taste layer: companies that figure out how to give agents their design language will ship products that feel intentional. Companies that don't will have increasingly interchangeable-looking software.
